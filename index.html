The space

LOAM is a modern two-bedroom apartment with a separate living room and kitchen. Each room has its own double bed, so it comfortably sleeps 6 — with an extra travel cot on request.

There is a fully equipped kitchen, fast 300 Mbps Wi-Fi, two 55" smart TVs and independent air conditioning in every room. Designed for couples, families and small groups who want to be in the middle of everything, yet come home to calm.
